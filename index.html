Frequently asked questions

What is included in a HEAL subscription?

All subscriptions include access to a HEAL clinician, secure messaging, telehealth visits, and medical oversight of your personalized protocols. Labs and medications are ordered through partner labs and pharmacies and may be billed separately depending on your plan.

Do you offer IV therapy or in-home visits?

No. HEAL is focused on virtual personalized care, prescriptions, and at-home therapies including peptides, GLP-1 medications, hormone optimization, and topical creams.

Do you take insurance?

We are a subscription-based practice and do not bill insurance directly. Upon request, we can provide documentation you can submit to your insurer, HSA, or FSA.

Where are you available?

Availability depends on state-specific regulations. Start by completing the questionnaire, and we’ll let you know if HEAL can treat you in your state or add you to our waitlist.

Is my information secure?

We use secure, encrypted platforms for telehealth, messaging, and record storage. HEAL follows HIPAA guidelines for protecting your health information.
